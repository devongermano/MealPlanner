(`python tests/test_capabilities.py regen`) on the pinned reference
environment (`refenv.is_reference_env() == True`: macOS arm64, CPython 3.14,

**The one behavior change that diffs the golden is M1.7 per-person serve_g
scaling** (including its emission-grid alignment fix, below). solo_lifter's
only person ("sol") targets 150p/65f/250c = **2185 Atwater kcal**, so
`engine.person_scale` = 2185 / 2500 (`SCALING["reference_kcal"]`) =
**0.874**, inside the [0.6, 1.8] clamp. Every serve band in every plate LP
is now the authored band x 0.874, re-aligned to the grid portions are
emitted on (unit grid for discrete components, whole grams otherwise — see
"Emission-grid alignment" under M1.7), which legally moves the per-day
optima and build_week's variety/re-plate choices. Observed golden deltas,
all consistent with that and nothing else:

- **menu unchanged** (same 6 components), `feasible: true`, `broke: {}`,
  `waste_perishable: 0` and `waste_total: 600` all unchanged;
- per-component weekly demand shifted (e.g. beef_braise 1600 -> 1375 g,
  chicken_grill 1534 -> 1616 g, rice_pot 5419 -> 5424 g): tighter scaled
  maxima (e.g. pork_roast's 400g authored max -> floor(400 x 0.874) = 349g
  effective) redistribute grams across days/components;
- batches follow demand (chicken_grill 4 -> 5, pork_roast 3 -> 4), so
  session minutes 288 -> 313 and purchase rows re-round to packs
  (chicken_mix 2000 g/4 packs -> 2500 g/5, pork_mix 1500/3 -> 2000/4);
- `session_plan` gained the **`"leftover": []`** key — the M1.8 cooked-
  leftover consumption report (schema addition; empty here because
  solo_lifter has no pantry, so it changes bytes but not behavior).

M1.0 and M1.8 changes do NOT otherwise touch the golden: the negative-miss
fallback and pin warnings only fire on structural failure / bad pins (the
golden pipeline has neither), tolerance validation strictly rejects more,
doctor-on-demand is CLI-only, and aging/leftovers are no-ops with no pantry.

## M1.7 — per-person serve_g scaling (PRD §8.1, Appendix B item 2)

`engine.SCALING` (PROVISIONAL, P9-labeled like SCORE_WEIGHTS):
`person_scale = kcal_of(targets) / 2500`, clamped to [0.6, 1.8].
`effective_serve_bounds` scales the authored band and re-aligns it to the
emission grid (min ceils, max floors); an inverted band falls back to the
UNSCALED bounds with a structured `serve_scale_unaligned` warning.
Consumers: plate LPs, carb headroom, volume floor, score_menu. Measuring
stick: the M1.6 real week.

**Emission-grid alignment (bug fix on the first M1.7 cut):** plate() emits
every portion as `int(round(val))`, but the first cut left CONTINUOUS
components' scaled bounds fractional (solo_lifter's 0.874 scale gave
refried_pintos hi = 456.799...), so a portion solved AT the scaled max
rounded up outside the band (457 > 456.8) — violating the per-person
bounds guarantee (repro: seed 3 of
test_portions_within_bounds_and_on_grid_property). `effective_serve_bounds`
now aligns continuous components to the whole-gram grid exactly as
discrete ones align to their unit grid: min ceils UP, max floors DOWN, so
every emitted integer portion stays inside the scaled band.

**Fixture re-sizing for M1.7** (capability fixtures author serve bands for
a 2,500-kcal reference eater; their sub-reference people now get shrunken
bands, so the designed scenarios needed re-sized authored maxima — each
annotated in its components.yaml):

- watery_vs_dense: watery serve_g.max 1600 -> 5500 so the 1,200-kcal
  person's 0.6-scaled band still admits the mass their carb target
  physically requires;
- valley (val 1,765 kcal, scale 0.706): stew max 650 -> 900, rice_pot max
  800 -> 1200 — days 0-5 stay feasible so day 6 is the ONLY (explained)
  hole, and the freezer flip bridges it;
- all_fatty (fatima 1,925 kcal, scale 0.77): sausage/belly max 600 -> 800,
  rice_pot max 700 -> 1000 — protein stays reachable (the fat-forcing
  trade needs fat dragged OVER while filling protein, not a carb short),
  and the lean-anchor variant flips feasible again;
- conflicting_exclusions (alice/bob 1,735 kcal, scale 0.694): base_stew
  max 600 -> 900, rice_pot max 700 -> 1000 — both people's days are
  feasible again, and the accent-split stays anti-vacuous (fat targets are
  unreachable without each person's non-excluded accents).

## M1.8 — pantry aging + cooked leftovers (PRD §8.1, Appendix B item 3)

`costing.age_pantry` consumes stock `acquired` dates against an explicit
`plan_date` (no wall clock): remaining raw life = keeps_days - age; stock
that cannot survive to any cook session is not deducted and is reported as
`stock_expiring_unused`; freezable stock past raw life freezes on arrival.
`costing.cooked_leftovers` joins the pantry `cooked` list into build_week
availability (strict window: `0 <= (plan_date + d) - cooked_date <
keeps_days`) and session_plan consumes oldest-first before fresh batches
(the new `leftover` report). `Pantry.cooked` left `RESERVED_FIELDS` (now
live). CLI: `--date YYYY-MM-DD` required whenever the pantry is non-empty.
